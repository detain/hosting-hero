## TOP-PROBLEMS
1. **Returning-visit hook is not in the bundle schema (line 1510).** The design law calls field 3 the difference between mastery content and one-off novelty, yet type-bundle.schema.json `required[]` carries verbs.dominant, verbs.transfers and handoverNote but no second-visit field — 2 of the 3 mandated per-entry fields are machine-enforceable, the campaign rhythm is not.
2. **The variety engine is proven at exactly 2 of ~34 types.** ADR-0005 makes 34 hosting types Ruleset Cards × eras (canonical count still OD-14 in DECISIONS-PENDING); only official:shared-web + official:game-servers shipped (4ce67fc), so this section's content is ~3% realized while its machinery (loader, three-change/verb-shift linter, G6 gate) is DONE.
3. **Visual Identity Kit ships half-resolved.** game-servers.json skin.palette has chord slots 4-5 as open todos ("awaiting the style-guide pass"), and no runtime per-type kit application exists yet (assetpack seed kit is one placeholder palette, b3a149d) — the "walking into a different building" law currently has exactly one playable chord.
4. **Scenario analogues are named but hollow (lines 1742, 1750).** launch-window / private-shard exist only as data hooks + i18n copy lines; a consumer reading game-servers.json scenarios could mistake the references for implemented level variants.
5. **The Indirect Control verbs the section leans on are missing from the door.** Colo's Negotiate (1618), CA's validate/log/prove (2500) and co-op's ask-not-assign (2878) have no representation among the 8 shipped PlayerVerbs (pipeline/intent-door.ts) — three flagship level designs need a verb-set extension, currently gated on ADR-0005's per-tier verb-set mechanism which only the hands-cap model exercises.
6. **Period levels have era machinery but no era grid.** The dial-up tutorial (2426) is called the game's best tutorial and the 1998-era toggle + i18n era variants shipped (17f3c8b), but there is no 1996 era row, no modem-port countable-concurrency board, and the Busy Wall grammar (praised as the canonical rendering for every hard slot limit) exists only in prose.
7. **Bulletproof hosting balance flag remains open in the spec (2241).** The doc's own ⚔️ note says as-written the line is the economically dominant strategy; all three proposed fixes are unratified and nothing shipped — if the type is ever authored, the balance question lands unprepared.
8. **Third-party-held scarce resources have no schema expression.** Email reputation (1779), CA trust-store standing (2500), payment-rail acceptance (2241, 2989) all need resources that cannot be bought back; bundle `scarce` supports only internal meters/sliders, so this recurring level archetype needs an engine contract before authoring.
