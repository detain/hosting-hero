### V1 — Two boards, one company

```
POSITIVE: editorial illustration, isometric diorama DNA, of two surfaces of one business laid side by side and revealed as the same object: on the left the Iron Board — a physical isometric data-center floor of dense alive racks, cool muted concrete and steel lit by their own equipment, cyan flow-beads moving along cables, green LED heartbeat flicker, a real cross-connect cable drooping with catenary sag; on the right The Book — a warm diegetic desk surface of contracts as tangible objects: blank folded rate sheets, signed lease tabs, an impaled AR spindle, a layered cash column, a pipeline funnel board with deal cards — every contract here represented by a small cable-and-socket form so the two boards rhyme; between them the two surfaces literally Fold into each other along a soft diagonal seam where the physical cable on the left becomes the contract-cable on the right, one continuous object crossing the boundary, its material losing specular warmth and gaining orthogonal precision as it passes — floor becoming grid, catenary becoming straight run; a single cyan flow-bead rides the cable unbroken across the fold, proving it is one system; composition balanced left-right with the fold as the spine, mood of quiet revelation that infrastructure and commerce are the same graph seen twice; limited desaturated palette with cyan and gold accents, hard readable silhouettes, must survive greyscale, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no UI captions.
NEGATIVE: text, letters, numbers, readable labels, contract words, watermark, UI captions, photorealism, neon, split-screen video look, busy clutter, two unrelated scenes with no connecting object.
size: 3072x1728
```
