The game's thesis in one image: a single pipe carrying both your customers and your attackers, and
a wall of your own defenses that grows so suspicious it starts throwing away the very people it
protects. Editorial cover-grade composition; iso-pixel diorama DNA retained.

### V1 — the cover: one pipe, two fates

```
POSITIVE: painterly editorial illustration with the DNA of 32-bit isometric pixel-art dioramas —
hard readable silhouettes, matte muted material palette of concrete grey, steel, putty beige and
black plastic, saturated colour reserved for living signal — wide 16:9 magazine-cover composition
of one enormous shared pipe running diagonally across the frame from a distant luminous horizon
edge toward a compact dark datacenter block in the near foreground: inside the pipe's transparent
upper half, a calm stream of small rounded cyan motes with tiny prop silhouettes (visitors, hex
#35E0E6) flows inward, while its lower half braids in angular magenta-flecked grit shapes (hex
#E04FD8) travelling with them, indistinguishable in density; midway, a tall layered wall of small
defense structures — gates, lattices, valve stations — straddles the pipe like a checkpoint city,
and the story is at its feet: a cluster of innocent cyan motes caught in its bars dissolving into
grey puffs that drift BACKWARD out of frame leaving a breadcrumb trail of tiny grey marks across
the floor, while a few clever magenta grit shapes slip through the same bars unchanged; the wall
glows a soft self-satisfied amber haze (hex #F2B133) around its posts; dramatic dusk lighting from
the horizon, room glow from the datacenter, one high window in the building showing storm weather;
deep atmospheric perspective, restrained film-grain texture, tasteful magazine-cover mood, painterly
rendering that still honors pixel-edge clarity on all key shapes, no text, no letters, no numbers,
no watermark, no UI captions, no logos
NEGATIVE: text, letters, numbers, watermark, photorealism, 3D render, cartoon slapstick, rainbow
palette, lasers, explosions, wires crossed everywhere, cluttered pipes, blue neon flood, faces
size: 3072x1728
aspect: 16:9
style: editorial-illustration
background: scene
variations: generate 4; the backward-drifting grey puffs must be the second thing you notice after
the pipe itself — that reversal is the concept
```

### V3 — the diagnostic: trail of grey marks

```
POSITIVE: moody editorial overhead-angle illustration with isometric pixel-art skeleton — a wide
calm view straight down along a long hall floor rendered in muted concrete tiles, the pipe running
its length as an inlaid dark channel with thin cyan stream-lines (hex #35E0E6) and a checkpoint
gate structure at its middle, steam-mist hugging one cold aisle; the painting's entire subject is
the floor: a scattered archaeology of small grey bounce-marks — dozens of tiny soft ash deposits in
fading greys across years of tiles, some fresh, some swept into corners, some overlapped and
trampled — tracing WHERE legitimate visitors gave up, clustering thick near the gate and thinning
along a detour path around an unlocked side valve; a single night-shift technician figure with a
```
