PROMO family, mixed 16:9 / 3:2 hero formats. These are the trailer-moment frames: spectacle with the game's ethics intact. The governing law of this file is the tone clause — commerce here is gratitude made visible, defense is a seaside promenade against weather, automation is a night nursery, never a sweatshop. Any seed that drifts toward dystopia, dehumanized crowds, or greed-aesthetic gets discarded no matter how beautiful.

**PICK CRITERIA:** tone law is the veto (§0.5): money frames must be checked for dystopia drift — if the crowd, the whale, or the coin-rain reads as exploitation at 128px thumbnail, discard the seed even if the full-res plate is gorgeous; the correct squint-test is that these images make a tired operator feel *seen*, not used. Series coherence: V5/V6/V7 are engineering epics (cold, structural), V1–V4/V8–V10 are domestic comedies (warm, bodily) — mix them in promos only with that temperature rhythm. Quiet Frame Test applies to V8 and V10, the two shots whose subject is silence. And one refusal honored throughout: no frame here shows an alert state as exciting — alert glow is always the incident's ghost (V7's outline, V10's clerestory gap), never the spectacle's fuel, per the form law that red is final and amber fills.
