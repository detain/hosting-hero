PROMO family, 3:2 scene plates. This is the game's death register rendered as eight standalone piece-works — each a different species of failure with its own visual physics. No comedy anywhere in this file (§9.3 protects the moment of loss); the tone is forensic, elegiac, occasionally awed. Dependency order, irreversible-versus-reversible, and procedural menace versus mechanical collapse are the three axes these eight must stay distinguishable on.

---
