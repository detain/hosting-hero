The end-movies of each business line, authored as sprite-sheet effect frames (start / middle /
aftermath columns per FX) on magenta so the engine can sequence them. These are the game's
fireworks budget — loud, but each obeys its hue's job.

### V1 — the rubber-band snap (replication lag catastrophe)

```
POSITIVE: three-column isometric pixel-art effect sequence sheet on a flat solid magenta #FF00FF
background, 32-bit iso game style, same short scene in all columns — two small dark database
cabinet objects facing each other across a lane, joined by a thick taut elastic data-strand drawn
in strained pale cyan (hex #35E0E6 desaturated under load): COLUMN ONE, the strand sagging happily
with one mote cruising it; COLUMN TWO, the right cabinet dragged backward, the strand stretched to
an absurd bowing length humming with tension-lines and vibration ghosts, tiny groan marks at both
anchor bolts, mote frozen mid-span unable to move, the whole image audibly creaking; COLUMN THREE,
the snap — strand recoiling into a whipping broken curve with its torn ends curling away from each
other, a burst of small pale shards at the midpoint, the right cabinet toppled a few degrees off
its feet with one drawer-spill of blank paper strips, both status lamps dead dark; hard readable
silhouettes through the motion, desaturated bodies, one restrained amber stress tint on the stretched
column's anchors only, crisp pixels, flat even lighting, no text, no letters, no numbers, no
watermark, no UI captions
NEGATIVE: text, letters, numbers, watermark, photorealism, explosion fireball, dramatic sparks,
red flood, rubber-duck cliches, speed lines everywhere, smoke clouds
size: 2048x1024
aspect: 2:1
style: pixel-art
background: magenta-key
variations: generate 4; the STRETCH column must physically hurt to look at — tension is the
telegraph; if column two looks like a normal cable, reseed
```

### V2 — the flood tide (volumetric overflow of the aisle)

```
POSITIVE: three-column isometric pixel-art catastrophe sheet on a flat solid magenta #FF00FF
background, 32-bit iso game style, one datacenter aisle fragment in escalating volumetric flood:
COLUMN ONE, the telegraph — a low horizon glow of deep violet-grey murk (hex #8F5FE8 kept as a thin
distant band, the unknown incoming) pressing at the aisle's far end while the near aisle stays
calm, one warning ripple on the floor tiles; COLUMN TWO, the tide cresting — a wall of dense
magenta-flecked slurry (hex #E04FD8 as foam-flecks and rim-light on a charcoal wave body, the wave
built from packed tiny angular grit shapes not water realism) pouring between two rack silhouettes,
mist bursting at the impact edges, cold tiles' low mist blown sideways, one rack's LED row caught
half-submerged in murk; COLUMN THREE, aftermath — the tide drained to a thin retreating film, the
aisle floor carrying a long deposit trail of spent magenta grit and dark sediment lines, two racks
dark with a single red fault pinprick each (hex #E23B3B, small, final), drifts of grit piled at the
containment curtain's base; hard readable silhouette drama, world materials muted so the tide owns
all colour, crisp pixels, lighting shifting cooler across the three columns, no text, no letters, no
numbers, no watermark, no UI captions
NEGATIVE: text, watermark, photorealism, literal ocean waves, water splashes, boats, rain, fire,
flooding office cliches, cartoon ducks, saturated rainbow, debris furniture
size: 2048x1024
aspect: 2:1
style: pixel-art
background: magenta-key
variations: generate 4; the violet→magenta handoff across columns encodes classification-arrives-
with-disaster — reject any frame where the horizon murk looks already-named hostile-magenta
```

### V3 — the dark cascade (breaker chain in physical order)

```
POSITIVE: three-column isometric pixel-art catastrophe sheet on a flat solid magenta #FF00FF
background, 32-bit iso game style, one power branch rendered as the game's copper circulatory
system — glowing copper conduit lines (hex #C97A45, thin luminous veins) forking from a heavy
feeder down a rack row feeding five small tap boxes with green pinprick lamps and lit fan-blur
racks above: COLUMN ONE, healthy — full copper glow, all lamps green, one fresh bright mote pulse
traveling the trunk; COLUMN TWO, the TRIP — a sharp open gap blown in the trunk at the first fork,
its breaker toggle arm thrown, the copper vein beyond the gap instantly drained of glow to dull
brown metal, the first two tap boxes dark with their lamps out and fan-blurs frozen mid-rotation as
hard-edged stopped blades, while the far side of the fork still shines — the darkness is traveling
IN ORDER along the physical path; COLUMN THREE, the cascade's shadow — the whole downstream family
dark, three more racks' LED fields extinguished, their glass-front fans stopped, a faint cooling
amber afterglow (hex #F2B133, two-pixel embers) dying on the last-affected panel, one lonely
upstream lamp still green above the gap marking where the world split; desaturated steel bodies,
the copper veins the only warmth, hard readable geometry of branching order even in greyscale by
glow-presence, crisp pixels, dimming light across columns, no text, no letters, no numbers, no
watermark, no UI captions
NEGATIVE: text, watermark, photorealism, sparks, arcs, fire, smoke, exploded panels, red alarm
wash, lightning, glowing error screens, tangled wires
size: 2048x1024
aspect: 2:1
style: pixel-art
background: magenta-key
variations: generate 4; grading criterion is ORDER — a viewer must be able to point at which racks
died first from the glow-map alone; that physical sequence is the spec's brutal two-second
animation
```

### V4 — the frost, the fray and the dead air (quiet terminations)

```
POSITIVE: sprite sheet of three related quiet-catastrophe effect clusters on a flat solid magenta
#FF00FF background, 32-bit iso game style, each on its own tiny scene fragment: (1) the FROST BLOOM
— a low storage shelf whose front is creeping over from its edges by angular pale crystalline
growth, sharp ice-like polygon shards stitching across the drawer faces in a slow geometric
contagion, the drawers behind the frost mid-motion frozen open, one warm interior light trapped and
dimming under the crystal, colors cold white-blue only in the frost, body stays matte graphite; (2)
the FRAYED LINE — a single taut glowing strand (pale voice-line teal) held at both ends by small
post insulators, caught at the exact moment its middle unravels into three curling loose fibres,
the signal motes on it spilling out sideways as fading dots, one end still trying to conduct a
straggled pair; (3) the DEAD AIR — a broadcast rack head with its big output cone, the light-sound
wave that should pour from the cone rendered as a thin dashed arc COLLAPSING inward, the cone's
mouth lit a dark warning red ring (hex #E23B3B as rim only), its audience-count lamp strip
extinguishing left to right in a decayed gradient of dark sockets; three moods — crystallized,
unwoven, silenced — all low-motion, dreadful through STILLNESS, hard silhouettes, desaturated,
crisp pixels, even dim lighting, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no UI captions
NEGATIVE: text, letters, numbers, watermark, photorealism, dramatic explosions, shattering glass,
tornado, melting, horror fog, lightning, neon concert lighting, snowflakes decorative
size: 2048x1024
aspect: 2:1
style: pixel-art
background: magenta-key
variations: generate 4; these are the catastrophes that arrive WITHOUT noise — each cluster must
pass the Quiet Frame test in reverse: something is deeply wrong in an otherwise still image
```

**PICK CRITERIA:** each FX stays inside its hue's JOB (magenta flood, copper cascade, white-teal
frost, red-as-final-rim only — §8.2 form laws); three-column sheets sequence-legible when shuffled
(telegraph→event→scar, §8.5); deposit/scar trails persist in the final column (Scar Map law §8.4 —
disasters must leave evidence).
