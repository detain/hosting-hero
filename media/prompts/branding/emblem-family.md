BRANDING family, in-game collectible artifacts. Seven heraldry stickers — one shield-of-arms per shipped hosting archetype, drawn as if each business line were an old trade guild, then rendered as die-cut vinyl stickers on the technical magenta key #FF00FF for extraction onto gear, placards, and achievement art. Heraldic law across the set: each coat uses its line's Five-Asset chord as its tincture palette, exactly one charge (central symbol) per shield, muted-dominant with a single state-color accent per Hue Ledger honesty, and a blank banner-ribbon under every shield reserved for the in-engine line name. The set must look like one engraving office produced all seven over twenty years — varying hand, shared toolbox. Sticker edges: die-cut white kiss-cut border with a subtle lift on one corner (peel life, since these get stuck on rack doors and laptops by players who earned them).

---

**PICK CRITERIA:** set law before singles — all seven must pass the wall test: pinned in a row (the placard-wall look), they read as one guild system: shared engraving hand, matched shield proportions, matched ribbon blank width, matched peel-corner direction; any lone-wolf seed is recast. One-charge law: exactly one central symbol each, verifiable at 48px; if two elements compete, one gets demoted to field texture. Palette honesty: each coat wears its line's Five-Asset chord and its state-accent sits where Hue Ledger meaning stays sane outside sim context (the amber on Email's lamp, the green on Backup's rim — never magenta, never violet, since those hues mean threat and unknown in-world and the player pins these to their own racks). Achievement-aesthetic check: shrink each to a 32px asset-tag size — the line most likely to be glanced at on a laptop lid, not studied; legibility of the single charge is the contract (§9.3: the cheapest possible storytelling must be instantly literate).
